Trading Strategies

Optimal ways to utilize MarketMind predictive AI models

Model Confirmation Strategy

The safest methodology. Wait for the Linear Regression trend to align perfectly with the News Sentiment score before taking action.

  • If Trend is UP & Sentiment > 60: Consider long positions.
  • If Trend is DOWN & Sentiment < 40: Consider exiting positions.
  • If signals conflict: Hold cash. Market is unpredictable.

Breakout Anticipation

Utilize the discrepancy between the Expected Open and Expected Close prices shown on the prediction dashboard.

  • Wide Margin: High volatility expected. Suitable for intraday.
  • Narrow Margin: Low volatility. Suitable for position building.
  • Verify margins against the historical 10-day average.

Mean Reversion Strategy

Use the AI Trend Line on the dashboard to spot overextensions where the actual price deviates significantly from the statistical prediction.

  • Price far above Trend: Strong probability of a short-term pullback (reversion).
  • Price far below Trend: Strong probability of a short-term rebound.
  • Best used in sideway or oscillating markets.

News Impact Trading

Leverage the dynamic "Top Highlights & News" feed on the home page combined with the stock-specific sentiment analysis.

  • Macro vs Micro: Check if a stock's negative sentiment is due to its own news or broader market conditions.
  • Contrarian Play: Buy fundamentally strong stocks that have temporary negative sentiment (Score < 30) due to broader market panic.
  • Monitor the NIFTY 50 dynamic news for macro shifts.
