What does "Bullish" mean here?

The Market Sentiment Graph visualizes the overall mood of financial news outlets evaluated by MarketMind's natural language processing engine using VADER analysis.

A "Bullish" reading (score > 60) indicates that the vast majority of recently published articles contain positive, optimistic terminology regarding economic growth, earnings, or tech advancements. This suggests that the broader market is likely to experience an upward trend in the near term as investor confidence is high. A "Bearish" score (score < 40) would indicate fear, pessimism, and potential downward market momentum.
